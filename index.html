Generative Print Series

Start with a blank file. End with a print on your wall. In between are ten chapters, each adding one layer to a single piece of generative art that you write yourself, in p5.js.

Your piece

Your sketch lives in the piece folder and gains one layer per chapter. Open the piece to see where it has got to.

Chapters are written one at a time by Claude, each after a session on the one before.
